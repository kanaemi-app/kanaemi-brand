# Kanaemi Trademark Policy

English | [日本語](TRADEMARKS.ja.md)

## Introduction

This document, the "Policy", sets out how the Kanaemi project (the "Project") lets others use its trademarks. Kanaemi's software is available under an open source license, but that copyright license does not include any right or license to use the Project's trademarks.

A trademark tells people where a product comes from and what to expect of it. Because an open source license lets anyone modify the software, the Project cannot be sure that a modified version would not mislead people if it were distributed under the same name. This Policy therefore describes when you may use the Marks.

This Policy does not try to limit lawful use of the Marks; it describes what the Project considers lawful use to be, so you can tell whether the Project will consider your use licensed or non-infringing. Any use that does not follow this Policy, and for which you have not received written permission, is not a use the Project has approved.

## Our commitment to open source principles

The Project wants the community to use the Marks, but in a way that keeps them meaningful as an indication of where the software comes from. This Policy tries to balance the need for the Marks to stay reliable with the community's wish to take full part in the Project.

## Trademarks covered by this Policy

This Policy covers the following marks, collectively the "Marks", whether or not they are registered:

1. The word marks (the "Word Marks"):

   | Mark      | Common descriptive name |
   | --------- | ----------------------- |
   | Kanaemi   | input method software   |
   | かなえみ  | input method software   |

2. The logos in the `logo/` directory of this repository, including the symbol (a keycap holding 「か」 and a smiling arc), the "kanaemi" wordmark, and their combinations (the "Logos").

## Universal considerations for all uses

Whenever you use one of the Marks, you must do so in a way that does not mislead anyone, directly or by omission, about exactly what they are getting and from whom. For example, you cannot say you are distributing Kanaemi software when you are distributing a modified version of it. You cannot use the Logos on your website in a way that suggests it is an official website or that the Project endorses it. You can, though, say that you like Kanaemi software, that you take part in the Kanaemi community, that you are providing an unmodified version of Kanaemi software, or that you wrote an article on how to use Kanaemi software.

In addition:

- You may not use the Marks in connection with distributing software if you are not complying with the copyright license of that software.
- You may not use or register the Marks, in whole or in part, as part of your own trademark, service mark, domain name, company name, trade name, product name or service name.
- You may not use an obvious variation, phonetic equivalent, translation, takeoff or abbreviation of the Marks for a similar or compatible product or service. The Project considers too similar, for example, any name for input method or text input software that contains "kanaemi", "かなえみ" or "カナエミ".
- You will not acquire any rights in the Marks, and any goodwill generated by your use of the Marks benefits the Project alone.

## Use for software

The universal considerations above also apply.

### Uses the Project considers non-infringing

#### Distributing unmodified software

When you redistribute an unmodified official release of Kanaemi, you are not changing its quality or nature, so you may keep the Word Marks and Logos the Project placed on it. This applies only to an official release that has not been changed in any way.

#### Distributing software you have built or modified

You may use the Word Marks, but not the Logos, to truthfully state where the software you distribute comes from — for example, "this software is derived from the source code of Kanaemi software." When you modify the software substantively, you become the origin of that version: give it your own name and logo, and do not use the Logos.

#### Statements about compatibility or derivation

You may use the Word Marks, but not the Logos, to truthfully describe how your software relates to Kanaemi. Put the Mark after a verb or preposition describing that relationship: "Bob's dictionary for Kanaemi software" is fine, "Bob's Kanaemi dictionary" is not. Other examples:

- [Your software] works with Kanaemi software
- [Your software] is compatible with Kanaemi software
- [Your software] for use with Kanaemi software

#### Showing community affiliation

You may use the Word Marks and Logos in themes, skins and similar decorations to show your support for the Project, as long as the use is non-commercial and clearly decorative rather than looking like the branding of a website or application.

## Use for non-software goods and services

The universal considerations above also apply.

### Uses the Project considers non-infringing

#### Websites

You may use the Word Marks and Logos on your website to show your support for the Project, as long as:

- your own branding or naming is more prominent than any of the Marks;
- the Logos link to the Project's website or repository;
- the site does not mislead people into thinking that your website, service or product is the Project's; and
- the site makes clear that you are not affiliated with or endorsed by the Project.

#### Publications and presentations

You may use the Word Marks in the titles of books, articles, videos and talks, and the Logos in illustrations within them, as long as the use does not suggest that the Project published, endorses or agrees with your work.

### Uses the Project grants a license for

#### User groups

You may use the Word Marks in the name of a user group, provided that the group focuses on Kanaemi software, provides any software or services without cost, does not make a profit, and charges for meetings only to cover the venue, food and drink. You still may not use or register the Marks as part of your own trademark, domain name, company name, product name or service name.

#### Promotional goods

You may make promotional goods for free giveaway that use the Logos exactly as provided in this repository.

### Uses the Project will likely consider infringing

- Using the Marks as part of a domain name or subdomain.
- Using the Marks on promotional goods for sale.

## Using the Logos

You may not change the Logos except to scale them proportionally. Do not add decorative elements, change their colors or proportions, distort them, or combine them with other logos. Use the variant that fits the context — dark, monochrome, small — from those provided in this repository, following the rules in [README](README.md).

## Using the Word Marks in text

- Write the Word Marks in their exact form: "Kanaemi" with an initial capital in English text, or "かなえみ". The lowercase "kanaemi" belongs to the wordmark logo only.
- Do not abbreviate, hyphenate or combine them with other words.

## Reporting misuse and asking for permission

If you see the Marks used in a confusing or misleading way, have questions about this Policy, or want to use the Marks in a way it does not cover, open an [issue in this repository](https://github.com/kanaemi-app/kanaemi-brand/issues).
